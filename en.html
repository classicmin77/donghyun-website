ABOUT DONGHYUN

Brands to markets,
culture to the world.

DONGHYUN introduces overseas brands to the Korean market and connects products with retail channels and consumers. Our experience includes a partnership with The Coca-Cola Company, overseas brand launches, placements in major Korean retail chains and licensing agreements.

Founded in 2015 with a focus on distribution, we added a culture and contents business in 2025. Today we cover food and cosmetics import/export, corporate gift sets, international cultural exchange, and the planning and management of publicly funded projects — tailored to each client's goals.

01 · BRAND, DISTRIBUTION & TRADE

Turning brand potential into market strength.

Drawing on brand launches, food and cosmetics trade and licensing deals, we bring products to the Korean market. With a track record in department stores, hypermarkets and duty-free shops, we run retail distribution and corporate gift-set supply.

  • Brand launches · LicensingLaunching overseas brands in Korea; brand and character licensing
  • Food & cosmetics import/exportTrade operations and product supply for food and cosmetics
  • Retail distribution · Pop-up storesDepartment store, hypermarket and duty-free placements; pop-up operations
  • Corporate merchandise · Gift setsSets built from about 500 items, with client-branded packaging and delivery

02 · CULTURE & CONTENTS

Bringing Korean culture to the world stage.

We plan international cultural exchange programmes spanning performance, film and hands-on experiences — working with local partners, booking artists and running events on site, with experience in planning and managing publicly funded projects.

  • International cultural exchangeConcerts, screenings and Korean culture programmes abroad
  • Concert planning · Artist bookingCrossover of classical music, Korean art song and gugak
  • Film & video contentMusic video submissions and screenings abroad; showcases and Q&As

Concerts & artist booking

  • Booking musicians, choirs and dancers

Film & video content

  • Service and performer contracts
  • Expense settlement and reporting
